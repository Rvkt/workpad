## 🌊 Stream of Thought / Reflections
Working on the [[04 Projects & Goals/Projects/Surfari Mobile Client|Surfari Mobile Client]] reinforced a fundamental truth: complex architectures fail not because of missing features, but because of uncontrolled state mutations and unmitigated latency.

Moving from ad-hoc solutions to formal [[02 Work/Decisions/ADR-001 Django Caching Layer with Redis|ADRs]] and permanent knowledge notes gives clarity to every PR. When our thinking is organized in the vault, our code reflects that clarity.

## 💡 Lessons, Insights & Mental Shifts
- **Key Insight:** Software architecture is the compression of business complexity into modular, isolated domains. Our Second Brain must mirror this exact principle.
